# Stellar Sleep

> Stellar Sleep (Y Combinator S23, backed by Initialized Capital) is a mobile app that helps people with chronic insomnia sleep better using psychology — a personalized program based on CBT-I (Cognitive Behavioral Therapy for Insomnia), the first-line, medication-free treatment recommended by sleep experts. Available for iOS and Android; published by Merlin Collective, LLC.

Stellar Sleep began at Harvard Innovation Labs in 2021 and launched publicly through Y Combinator's Summer 2023 batch. It is a consumer digital-health product: there is no public developer portal, API, SDK, or MCP server. Clinicians and employers can partner with Stellar Sleep through dedicated programs; integrations with clinical systems are handled by the Stellar Sleep team case by case rather than through a self-serve API.
